# Safety and etiquette

- Use only public board APIs (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby) and public careers pages. One request per second per host. Send an honest `User-Agent`.
- Do not scrape sites that forbid it in their terms (several large job aggregators do). Use their official exports or ask the user to paste results.
- Never log in on the user's behalf, store their passwords, or ask for them.
- Never submit applications. Never fill work-authorization, sponsorship, EEO, disability or criminal-history fields.
- Never bypass CAPTCHAs or rate limits.
- Keep personal data (profiles, application logs, resumes) out of git. `profiles/<name>/` and `out/` are ignored by default for anything except the shipped examples.
- The user is responsible for the accuracy of what they submit.
