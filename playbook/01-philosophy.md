# The approach: top-down

Most people search bottom-up: type a job title into a search box and scroll. You get whatever the algorithm shows, it is the same list everyone else sees, and you read hundreds of listings at employers you do not care about.

Do it top-down instead.

- **Start from employers, not listings.** Decide which companies you would be proud to join, in tiers. Then watch their job boards directly.
- **Rank before you read.** A cheap keyword score sorts postings first. Spend your attention on the top of the list.
- **You review, you submit.** Tools find and rank. You read every posting and press every submit button. This protects you from bad auto-applications and keeps your voice yours.
- **Keep a record.** Write down what you applied to and when, so follow-ups and weekly reviews are easy.
- **Treat networking as part of the search.** An application plus a short, honest message to a person on the team beats either alone.

**Ask your agent:** "Explain the top-down method back to me, then ask me about my target role and cities."
