# Weekly routine

1. **Scan** (10 minutes): re-run fetch and rank. Look only at what is new.
2. **Triage** (20 minutes): read the top new postings, mark apply, maybe or skip.
3. **Apply** (as many as you can do well): read, tailor, proofread, submit by hand, log it.
4. **Reach out** (15 minutes): one short, specific message to someone at a tier 1 or 2 company.
5. **Update the Applications tab**: move each application to its new status, and follow up on anything with no reply after about two weeks.
6. **Adjust**: once a month, prune companies that never produce matches and add new ones.

**Ask your agent:** "Run this week's scan and show me only new postings at tier 1 and 2 companies."
