# Rank and triage

The ranked list sorts by **tier first**, then **keyword score**, then **newest**.

- Positive keywords raise a posting's score. Title matches count more than description matches.
- Negative keywords lower it, so roles that are obviously off target sink.
- Filters remove postings outside your cities or above your level.
- Every row shows **why** it scored as it did. If a result looks wrong, fix the keywords, not the list.

Triage the top of the list: open each posting, read it, and mark it *apply*, *maybe* or *skip*. Do not apply to a posting you have not read.

**Ask your agent:** "Rank this week's postings and explain the top five. Which keywords should I change?"
